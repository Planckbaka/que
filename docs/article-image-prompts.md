## attention

Use case: scientific-educational. Create a square editorial educational illustration for a technical blog about causal Attention. Vintage mystery-book print aesthetic, ivory paper #eee8d8, near-black ink #191610, restrained dark vermilion #a33024, subtle halftone, crisp large typography, generous whitespace, hard-edged paper-cut geometry. The visual must teach weighted reading, not merely decorate. Title exactly "CAUSAL ATTENTION". Main diagram: one red query card labelled "Q" at left; three vertically stacked past/current key-value cards in center labelled "K1 / V1", "K2 / V2", "K3 / V3"; arrows from query toward keys, then from the corresponding values converge on a single large card at right labelled "OUTPUT". Three value-to-output arrows of different thickness suggest different weights, not hard selection. Below center separated by a vertical dashed boundary a grey card labelled "FUTURE" with a crossed-out connection showing future values are masked. Bottom caption exactly "Score keys. Weight values. Mask the future." Small caption "Conceptual view of one query". No numerical probabilities, equations, matrix grids, logos, ornamental birds or extra labels. Legible at narrow mobile width. Draw clean unambiguous arrows with arrowheads. The future card must not connect to output.
