The witness memorized every detail of the training room — every chair, every scuff on the floor, every face on the jury. Under direct examination the testimony was flawless. Then we moved the trial to a different room, and the witness could not tell a chair from a table.

This file remains sealed: the cross-examination is still being written, and the case is not ready for the archive wall. It exists here so the draft pipeline has a real body to guard — prerender skips it, the archive will not list it, and direct visits meet a sealed-file notice.
